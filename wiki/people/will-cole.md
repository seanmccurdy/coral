# Will Cole

Will Cole is a functional-medicine telehealth practitioner and author whose organizing frame is that chronic symptoms — brain fog, fatigue, anxiety, depression, digestive dysfunction — signal cellular dysfunction, particularly what he calls cellular confusion: cells losing fidelity to their differentiated identity (exdifferentiation, in his rendering), which he grounds loosely in epigenetic information-loss framings of aging and in organ-specific aging clock research. He is a clinician-communicator, not a researcher; his claims mix described peer-reviewed findings, clinical anecdote, commercial-adjacent supplement claims, and explicitly unevidenced speculation, and this page's job is to keep those strata separated. (@maxlugavere (Max Lugavere) — "The Hidden Cause of Brain Fog, Fatigue & Poor Metabolic Health - Dr. Will Cole", 2026-09-02, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXrrcZjx3c4))

Attributed positions, by evidential stratum:

- **Anchored in described research (accuracy of his rendering not verified here):** organ-specific aging — he cites Stanford-associated work published in Nature finding one in five adults over 50 with at least one organ aging faster than the rest, with accelerated brain aging raising neurodegenerative risk and accelerated heart aging raising cardiac risk, plus 2026 follow-up work at cell-type resolution; and birth-cohort aging acceleration — UK Biobank-based work described as showing people born after 1965 aging faster than earlier cohorts at the same age. Both are developed with caveats at [[biological-age-biomarkers]]. (@maxlugavere (Max Lugavere) — "The Hidden Cause of Brain Fog, Fatigue & Poor Metabolic Health - Dr. Will Cole", 2026-09-02, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXrrcZjx3c4))
- **Clinical practice positions:** symptoms are signal, not baseline — of brain fog, fatigue, anxiety, and depression he says "That's not normal. That's just common."; gut trouble shows up as bowel-habit abnormality, post-meal bloating, and food reactivity long before diagnosis; a time-limited elimination diet with deliberate reintroduction (a "proverbial cast" for a stressed gut) beats both perpetual restriction and untargeted supplementation. He explicitly warns that permanent elimination diets destroy the hormetic tolerance that plant-compound diversity builds. [[food-patterns-and-gut-ecology]] (@maxlugavere (Max Lugavere) — "The Hidden Cause of Brain Fog, Fatigue & Poor Metabolic Health - Dr. Will Cole", 2026-09-02, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXrrcZjx3c4))
- **Mechanistic framings shared with the longevity mainstream but stated with more confidence than the evidence carries:** chronic overnutrition keeps mTOR chronically high and starves autophagy/mitophagy of what he calls "cellular siestas"; cyclical fasting, polyphenols, and other pulsed hormetic stressors restore repair; xenohormesis (stressed plants make compounds that up-regulate human cellular resilience). Compare the calibrated versions at [[mtor-and-rapamycin]], [[autophagy-and-lysosomal-quality-control]], and [[caloric-restriction-and-meal-timing]]. (@maxlugavere (Max Lugavere) — "The Hidden Cause of Brain Fog, Fatigue & Poor Metabolic Health - Dr. Will Cole", 2026-09-02, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXrrcZjx3c4))
- **Speculative or unevidenced, some flagged as such by Cole himself:** an EMF–mold interaction in which electromagnetic fields make mold release more mycotoxins and people more receptive to them — he concedes "There's no strong data on that" and offers clinical impression only; a "cellular fragility syndrome" affecting up to one in three people, remediated by the fatty acid C15 (pentadecanoic acid) from hard cheeses — language tracking commercial C15-supplement marketing; spiritual practice lowering inflammation and mortality via cellular effects (observational associations with heavy confounding); "Ozempic personality" — GLP-1 drugs blunting reward and relationships, sourced to direct messages and explicitly offered as something that "should be explored" rather than shown. He also uses growth-hormone-adjacent peptides personally while cautioning that longevity researchers oppose chronically elevated growth signaling ([[experimental-peptides]]). None of these belong in practice guidance. Cole's moderating instinct is worth recording alongside the speculation: he cautions against orthorexic over-restriction, against stressing over exposures irrelevant to one's own labs, and against fear-driven wellness culture — "Stressing about healthy things isn't good for your health." ## Practical implications

- Treat Cole's material as hypothesis-generating clinical narrative. His actionable overlaps with better-evidenced pages (polyphenol-rich foods, fiber and fermented-food progression, time-restricted or cyclical eating, resistance to perpetual elimination) should be adopted from those pages' calibrated versions, not from his stronger cellular-confusion framing. (@maxlugavere (Max Lugavere) — "The Hidden Cause of Brain Fog, Fatigue & Poor Metabolic Health - Dr. Will Cole", 2026-09-02, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXrrcZjx3c4))
- Discount the EMF–mold, C15 fragility-syndrome, and spirituality-as-cellular-medicine claims to their evidential weight: anecdote, commercial claim, and confounded association respectively. (@maxlugavere (Max Lugavere) — "The Hidden Cause of Brain Fog, Fatigue & Poor Metabolic Health - Dr. Will Cole", 2026-09-02, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXrrcZjx3c4))

## Gaps & open questions

- Does his rendering of the organ-aging and birth-cohort studies match the primary papers' populations, endpoints, and effect sizes?
- Do elimination-then-reintroduction protocols outperform simple graded fiber/fermented-food escalation in any controlled comparison?
- Is there any controlled evidence for pentadecanoic acid (C15) supplementation changing membrane integrity or clinical outcomes in humans?
