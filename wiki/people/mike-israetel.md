# Mike Israetel

Mike Israetel is an exercise scientist, competitive bodybuilder, and co-founder of Renaissance Periodization. His distinctive contribution in the current corpus is bringing explicit trade-off accounting into fitness advice: every physique goal is priced in energy, mood, libido, time, and health cost, and rational pursuit means knowing the price before paying it — including his own disclosed choice to use anabolic steroids at a knowing cost to lifespan. His training doctrine is deliberately anti-complexity: consistency, a simple repeatable program, sound technique, objective progression (add small load or a repetition on a schedule rather than training by feel), and patience outrank any programming refinement; auto-regulation is reserved for experienced lifters with baseline performance data. Attributed positions, several of them contrarian within fitness culture:

- **Vanity is a legitimate, effective motive.** Changing appearance is a valid route to better self-image, not a moral failure to be hidden behind health justifications; the toxicity lies in comparison to others, comparison to perfection, and uninformed escalation, not in the motive itself.
