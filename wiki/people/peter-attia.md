# Peter Attia

Physician and host of The Drive podcast, focused on the applied science of
longevity. Runs a clinical practice that aggressively manages
cardiovascular and metabolic risk (Peter Attia MD — "402 ‒ NMR blood
analysis: how mortality risk and more can be assessed from a single blood
sample", 2026-08-03, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMbghqZ1iXI)).

## Positions

- **Peptides**: skeptical of the gray-market wellness peptide ecosystem,
  not of peptide science itself. Argues "do peptides work?" is the wrong
  question — each molecule must be run through a five-question evidence
  framework, and most gray-market peptides fail it. Places [[bpc-157]] in
  his "scientifically unsupported" bucket and [[cjc-1295]] in the
  "plausible biology, no human outcome evidence" bucket (Peter Attia MD —
  "403 ‒ Peptides: separating scientific promise from marketing hype",
  2026-08-10, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km_01CLEkYU)). See
  [[peptides]] and [[gray-market-peptides]].
- **Lipidology**: long-time advocate of particle-number metrics (LDL-P,
  ApoB) over LDL cholesterol; began following [[jim-otvos]]'s work in May
  2011 after an introduction by Tom Dayspring, and treats elevated
  LDL-C/ApoB/LDL-P as a causal driver to be treated immediately rather
  than waiting for disease — analogous to telling a smoker to quit before
  cancer appears (Peter Attia MD — "402 ‒ NMR blood analysis: how
  mortality risk and more can be assessed from a single blood sample",
  2026-08-03, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMbghqZ1iXI)). See
  [[ldl-particle-number]].
- **Environmental exposures**: real but, for most people, sitting well
  below sleep, exercise, nutrition, blood pressure, lipids, and metabolic
  health in the hierarchy of what matters; favors an 80/20 approach of a
  few low-burden, high-return interventions rather than trying to
  eliminate every exposure (Peter Attia MD — "Environmental pollution and
  longevity: air pollution, noise, light, EMFs, & more (AMA 87 sneak
  peek)", 2026-07-19, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDxAiqfy4-g)).
  See [[environmental-pollution]].
- **Testimonials and placebo**: anecdotes can generate hypotheses but
  cannot establish effect size, dose, responders, or harms; RCTs exist to
  separate the drug from the story around it (Peter Attia MD — "403 ‒
  Peptides: separating scientific promise from marketing hype",
  2026-08-10, [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km_01CLEkYU)). See
  [[placebo-effect]].
