## P1 — clinical and whole-system coverage

- [x] P1 Visible skin and facial aging — create one foundational chapter separating intrinsic aging from photoaging and explaining epidermal/barrier change, dermal collagen and elastin remodeling, pigment and vascular change, subcutaneous fat redistribution, retaining ligaments, muscle activity, and craniofacial remodeling. Map each visible feature to its anatomical compartment, distinguish prevention from partial improvement, and state which changes topical products cannot plausibly reach.
- [x] P1 Visible-aging outcomes and evidence — define validated and clinically interpretable endpoints for wrinkles, dyspigmentation, erythema, laxity, texture, hydration, barrier function, patient satisfaction, blinded photographic assessment, histology, and device measurements. Separate statistically detectable change from visible or patient-important benefit, and audit dependence on sponsor-selected images and scales.
- [x] P1 Topical visible-aging interventions — synthesize photoprotection and prescription retinoid evidence first, then assess niacinamide, vitamin C and other antioxidants, alpha-hydroxy acids, peptides, growth-factor or polynucleotide products, moisturizers, and combination formulas. Require finished-product, stability, concentration, delivery, duration, adverse-effect, skin-tone, and independent-replication evidence; consolidate unsupported novelty rather than creating an ingredient page for each claim.
- [x] P1 Procedures, injectables, and structural facial aging — reorganize procedural coverage by target layer and indication: pigment and vessels; resurfacing and dermal remodeling; dynamic lines; volume loss; laxity and descent. Compare lasers, IPL, chemical peels, microneedling, radiofrequency and ultrasound devices, botulinum toxins, fillers and biostimulators, fat grafting, and surgery using durability, absolute visible benefit, skin-tone applicability, operator dependence, downtime, adverse events, reversibility, and evidence quality. Do not turn product- or device-specific settings into protocols without authoritative support.

## Queue rules

- Preserve both kinds of attribution: episode citations document provenance; scholarly references document evidentiary support.
- Complete an item only after its chapter is integrated into the wiki, connected with wikilinks, and checked against the reference rules in `_index.md`.
- If an item is too broad for one pass, add narrower unchecked items directly beneath it and leave the parent unchecked.
- Add newly discovered cross-wiki gaps here with a priority and a concrete research question.
