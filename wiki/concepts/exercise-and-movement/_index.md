# Exercise and movement

Begin with [[exercise-program-design]] for outcome-first selection of training variables. Build the major capacities through [[cardiorespiratory-fitness]], [[skeletal-muscle-hypertrophy]], and [[daily-movement-mobility-and-pain]], then use the remaining chapters for programming, technique, tissue tolerance, sport development, and life-stage constraints. [[spinal-traction-and-fascial-decompression]] covers the traction side of soft-tissue work, which is mechanically distinct from the compression techniques more commonly described. Specific exercises are tools for delivering an appropriate stimulus; they are not independent longevity mechanisms.
