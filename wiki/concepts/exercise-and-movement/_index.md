Use the remaining chapters for programming, technique, tissue tolerance, sport development, and life-stage constraints. [[spinal-traction-and-fascial-decompression]] covers the traction side of soft-tissue work, which is mechanically distinct from the compression techniques more commonly described. Specific exercises are tools for delivering an appropriate stimulus; they are not independent longevity mechanisms.
