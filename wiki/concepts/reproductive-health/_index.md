# Reproductive health

This section connects reproductive physiology, aging, fertility, sexual health, and disease. [[oocyte-aneuploidy-and-reproductive-aging]] and [[ovarian-aging-and-tissue-cryopreservation]] explain reproductive aging and preservation limits. [[endometriosis]] and [[adenomyosis]] cover related but distinct uterine and extrauterine disease processes. [[low-energy-availability-and-menstrual-function]] connects energy availability to reproductive-axis conservation, while [[male-fertility-and-exogenous-testosterone]] explains endocrine feedback in male fertility. [[pornography-motivation-and-sexual-health]] separates arousal, motive, media scripts, consent, and functional outcomes. [[phimosis-and-paraphimosis]] distinguishes chronic foreskin restriction from an ischemic emergency; [[genital-modification-and-sexual-function]] compares piercing, enlargement, and surgery through function and reversibility; [[genital-restraint-device-safety]] explains constriction, pressure, occlusion, release planning, consent, and emergency recognition; [[bladder-filling-and-voiding-behavior]] explains storage, emptying, learned urgency, and symptom-aware monitoring; [[kidney-stones]] covers supersaturation chemistry, evidence-based prevention (fluid, sodium, protein, selective oxalate, citrate), 24-hour urine testing, and the shift toward aspiration and office-based lithotripsy. [[vulvovaginal-anatomy-and-hormone-sensitivity]] teaches the hormone-responsive vulvar tissues, vestibular pain, and the myths about what sex changes; [[genitourinary-syndrome-of-menopause]] covers postmenopausal tissue change, local hormone therapy, and the treatment gap; [[sexual-desire-arousal-and-aging]] separates desire, brain arousal, and genital arousal and explains how aging shifts stimulation needs and sexual scripts. [[vulvovaginal-candidiasis]] explains yeast-infection ecology — microflora balance, antibiotic, glycemic, hormonal, and moisture risk factors — against the diet and probiotic myths, and defines when recurrence ends self-treatment. [[premature-ejaculation]] separates the clinical control–bother–latency diagnosis from stopwatch self-diagnosis, teaches the set-point-plus-anxiety-loop model, and organizes behavioral, psychological, pelvic-floor, topical, and off-label pharmacologic treatment with expectation-setting. [[benign-prostatic-hyperplasia]] explains how prostate enlargement produces obstructive and storage urinary symptoms, why frequency is multi-causal (diabetes, sleep apnea, learned voiding), and which metabolic and dietary associations are modifiable. [[female-ejaculation-and-squirting]] covers the mixed bladder-and-Skene's-gland origin of expelled fluid, its ~40% prevalence against media-driven universality expectations, and the physiological orgasm signature that fluid is not. [[polycystic-ovary-syndrome]] teaches the syndrome's two-of-three diagnostic structure, the hyperinsulinemia→theca-cell androgen loop, and its reframing from a fertility problem to a lifelong cardiometabolic trajectory.
