# Diagnostics and screening

This section distinguishes risk assessment, early detection, diagnosis, and improved outcomes. Start with [[proactive-health-monitoring]], then compare established organ-specific programs in [[breast-cancer-screening]] and [[colorectal-cancer-prevention-and-screening]] with the emerging evidence and unresolved harms in [[multi-cancer-early-detection]]. A test improves health only through an accurate result followed by an effective and proportionate action.
