# Diagnostics and screening

This section distinguishes risk assessment, early detection, diagnosis, and improved outcomes. Start with [[proactive-health-monitoring]], then use [[continuous-glucose-monitoring]] for the population-specific evidence on CGM outside insulin-treated diabetes: dynamic measurement, short-term biofeedback, normal excursions, prediabetes, and the missing durable outcomes. Compare established organ-specific programs in [[breast-cancer-screening]] and [[colorectal-cancer-prevention-and-screening]] with the emerging evidence and unresolved harms in [[multi-cancer-early-detection]]. [[cancer-screening-and-overdiagnosis]] supplies the cross-cutting arithmetic — overdiagnosis, the treatment cascade, and the prostate, thyroid, and ovarian examples where detection rose without mortality falling. [[blood-marker-variability-and-reference-change]] covers the measurement layer beneath any repeated lab: analytical and biological variation, reference change values, and which markers can be tracked at all. A test improves health only through an accurate result followed by an effective and proportionate action.
