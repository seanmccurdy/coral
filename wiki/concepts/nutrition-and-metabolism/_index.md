# Nutrition and metabolism

Start with [[nutrition-evidence-and-personalization]] for evidence interpretation and substitution logic. [[dietary-fiber]], [[dietary-fat-quality-and-cardiovascular-risk]], [[dietary-protein-and-cardiovascular-risk]], [[free-sugars-and-glycemic-response]], and [[food-patterns-and-gut-ecology]] explain major food-to-physiology pathways; the protein chapter separates quantity, source, substitution, ApoB, muscle, and anabolic-steroid confounding while preserving the unresolved very-high-intake signal in older adults. [[coffee-preparation-and-health]] applies the same matrix logic to caffeine, polyphenols, diterpenes, filtration, timing, and additives. [[cheese-and-mortality]], [[tomato-products-and-lycopene]], [[anthocyanins-and-cardiovascular-health]], [[heme-iron-and-colorectal-cancer]], [[oats-and-cardiovascular-health]], and [[nut-consumption-and-mortality]] apply those principles to specific foods and nutrients and distinguish mechanisms, observational outcomes, and causal claims. [[gout-and-urate-lowering]] explains purine turnover, crystal deposition, flare prophylaxis, and treat-to-target therapy. [[iodine-and-cognitive-development]] covers the iodine–thyroid–brain pathway, the fortification natural experiment, declining population intake, and the conflict between the salt vehicle and sodium-reduction evidence. [[culinary-mushrooms-and-fungal-nutrition]] extends the food-specific set to the fungal kingdom — vitamin D photochemistry, ergothioneine, beta-glucans, the gut mycobiome, and preparation. [[nutrient-density-and-food-profiling]] covers how nutrient-density scoring algorithms are built, why their design choices produce or violate common-sense rankings, the sodium-to-potassium processing proxy, and the policy surfaces a validated score can act on. [[ultra-processed-food]] covers the industrial food system end to end: monoculture and subsidy economics, engineered hyperpalatability and the food-addiction question, identification heuristics and their decay, and cooking as the counter-strategy. Use [[satiety-oriented-diet-design]] and [[evolutionary-mismatch-and-weight-regulation]] for appetite and adherence, [[paleolithic-diets-and-modern-paleo]] for what the archaeological record actually shows about ancestral eating and why the modern paleo diet's grain, legume, and dairy exclusions fail both the history and the health evidence, and [[supplement-evidence-and-safety]] for evaluating concentrated products.
