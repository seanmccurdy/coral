# Aging biology

This section explains the molecular and cellular maintenance systems that change with age. Begin with [[aging-dynamics-and-resilience]] for the system-level problem, then study damage and maintenance through [[genomic-instability-and-dna-repair]], [[loss-of-proteostasis]], [[autophagy-and-lysosomal-quality-control]], and [[mitochondrial-dysfunction]]. Continue through cell-state and regenerative consequences in [[cellular-senescence]], [[stem-cell-exhaustion]], [[telomere-biology]], and [[epigenetic-alterations-and-reprogramming]]. Use [[biological-age-biomarkers]] to separate measurement from mechanism and [[longevity-intervention-prioritization]] to translate the biology cautiously.

These mechanisms interact; none is established as a single master cause. Their integration belongs in [[aging-model]].
